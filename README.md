# 🌿 Aeolus Green Energy - Power BI Dashboard

A Business Intelligence solution developed for **Aeolus Green Energy** to provide actionable insights into renewable energy operations using **Microsoft Power BI**. This project focuses on climate analytics, energy production monitoring, and CRM service performance to support data-driven decision-making.


## 📌 Project Overview

This project was developed as part of the **Business Intelligence and Business Analytics** module.

The solution combines multiple datasets to help stakeholders monitor renewable energy performance, operational efficiency, and customer service metrics through interactive Power BI dashboards.

The dashboards provide insights into:

- Climate trends affecting renewable energy production
- Energy generation and distribution efficiency
- Customer service operations and SLA performance
- Revenue leakage analysis
- Operational KPIs for business decision-making


## 📊 Dashboards Included

### 1️⃣ Dublin Climate Analytics Dashboard

This dashboard analyzes historical weather patterns using Open-Meteo data.

**Key Features**

- Average Temperature
- Average Wind Speed
- Humidity Analysis
- Rainfall Distribution
- Wind Direction Analysis
- Temperature Category Distribution
- Monthly and Yearly Trends
- Interactive Filters


### 2️⃣ Energy Production & Efficiency Dashboard

This dashboard monitors renewable energy generation and operational efficiency.

**Key Features**

- Total Energy Generated
- Total Energy Delivered
- Average Efficiency
- Revenue Analysis
- Energy Curtailment
- Generation Trends
- Zone-wise Energy Distribution
- Sensor Status Monitoring
- Energy Type Comparison


### 3️⃣ Service Operations & SLA Dashboard

Designed for operational monitoring and customer service management.

**Key Features**

- SLA Compliance
- Case Volume Analysis
- Resolution Time
- Revenue Leakage
- Engineer Performance
- Issue Type Distribution
- County-wise Case Analysis
- Monthly Case Trends


## 📁 Dataset

## 📊 Data Model

The project follows a **Star Schema** architecture consisting of:

### Fact Tables

- FACT_WEATHER
- FACT_DISTRIBUTION
- FACT_CRM

### Dimension Tables

- DIM_DATE
- DIM_ZONE
- DIM_SITE
- DIM_ACCOUNT
